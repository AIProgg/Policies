1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to Countsy, an Android counting application available on Google Play under the package identifier com.gisc.counter.

Countsy is published on Google Play by GISC Pvt. Ltd and is operated by GLOBIANCE INTELLIWEB SOFTWARE CONSULTANCY PRIVATE LIMITED (“GISC,” “we,” “our,” or “us”).

This policy explains how information is handled when you install or use Countsy.

2. Information We Collect

Countsy does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data.

The application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photographs, or payment information.

3. App Data and Counter Activity

Countsy is designed to perform its counting functions locally on the user’s device. Counter interactions are not transmitted to GISC or any external server.

4. Device Permissions

Countsy does not request special device permissions and does not access sensitive device features such as:

  • Location
  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts
  • Photos, videos, or other personal files
  • Phone or SMS information
  • Health information
  • Financial or payment information

5. Internet Access

Countsy is designed to work offline and does not require an internet connection for its counting functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

Countsy does not use third-party advertising networks, analytics services, tracking technologies, or external services that collect personal data from users.

7. Data Sharing and Selling

Because Countsy does not collect personal or sensitive user data, we do not sell, rent, disclose, or share such data with third parties.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

GISC does not retain personal data through Countsy because the application does not collect or transmit personal data to us.

Any locally stored application data can be removed by clearing the application’s storage or uninstalling Countsy from the device.

9. Children’s Privacy

Countsy does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, Countsy does not create user profiles for children.

10. Data Security

Countsy is designed to minimize privacy risks by operating locally and avoiding the collection or transmission of personal data. Users should continue to protect their devices using appropriate security measures.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy
